## Hi there 👋
My name is Verena and I am currently a CONICET Assistant Researcher at the [Northeast Botany Institute](https://ibone.unne.edu.ar/inicio) and North-eastern National University. My research focus on how uniparental reproductive strategies (like apomixis or selfing) “help” in the origin of agamic and polyploid complexes in grasses.

🎓I gained my Ph.D. in Biology at the Cordoba National University when working in the [Genetics and Improvement of Forage Species Lab](https://ibone.unne.edu.ar/investigacion/genetica). During my Ph.D, I studied the population structure of Paspalum (Poaceae) species with divergent genetic systems. 

🌾🧬Genetic systems in Paspalum comprise a varying number of ploidy levels (from 2x to 16x) and contrasting reproduction modes (sexuality and/or apomixis) and mating systems (selfing vs outcrossing vs mixed-mating systems).
